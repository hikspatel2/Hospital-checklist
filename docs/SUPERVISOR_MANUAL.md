# Supervisor User Manual
## Sheth L.G. General Hospital, Ahmedabad
### Facility Inspection & Floor Supervisor Operational Guide

---

## 1. Introduction & Supervisor Role

As a **Hospital Facility Supervisor** at Sheth L.G. General Hospital, your primary responsibility is ensuring that hospital wards, ICUs, corridors, washrooms, and public zones maintain high standards of cleanliness, biomedical waste disposal, and facility maintenance.

You will use this web portal on your mobile smartphone or hospital tablet during each shift.

---

## 2. Accessing the System on Mobile

1. Open the web browser on your phone (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge).
2. Go to: `https://shethlg.com/login` (or the hospital's official web address).
3. Enter your assigned email address and password.
4. **Important**: When prompted by your phone browser:
   * **Allow Camera Access**: Required for scanning Ward QR codes.
   * **Allow Location / GPS Access**: Required for verifying physical presence at the hospital during shift check-in.

---

## 3. Shift Check-In (QR Code & GPS Geofence)

Before starting inspections, you must check in for your assigned shift.

### How to Check In:
1. Walk to your assigned hospital ward or nursing station where the official **Ward QR Code Poster** is mounted.
2. Tap **Attendance** > **Check In** on your mobile screen.
3. Tap **Scan QR Code** to activate your camera, or point your camera at the QR code on the wall.
4. The system will automatically capture your GPS coordinates.
5. Tap **Submit Check-In**.
6. A green confirmation badge will appear: `Check-in Verified - Within Hospital Campus`.

### What to do if GPS fails or says "Outside Campus":
* Ensure **Location Services (GPS)** is turned on in your phone's main settings.
* If indoors under thick concrete, step near a window or corridor for 10 seconds to allow the GPS chip to acquire satellite lock.
* If your phone still cannot get a location, report to the Facility Admin immediately so a manual supervisor check-in override can be recorded.

---

## 4. Today's Inspection Queue

Once checked in, your homepage displays **Today's Inspections**:
* **Card View**: Each card represents a scheduled inspection for a ward on your floor.
* **Status Badges**:
  * ⚪ **Scheduled**: Ready to be inspected during this shift.
  * 🟡 **In Progress**: An inspection you have already opened.
  * 🟢 **Completed**: Successfully audited and submitted.
  * 🔴 **Overdue**: Approaching or past scheduled cutoff time.

---

## 5. Conducting a Ward Inspection Step-by-Step

1. Walk into the target ward (e.g., *Sardar Patel Block - 3rd Floor - Pediatric Ward*).
2. Tap the **Start Inspection** button on the inspection card.
3. The interactive checklist form will load:
   * **Pass / Fail Items**: Tap **Pass** if compliant, or **Fail** if non-compliant (e.g., *Bio-medical waste bins properly color-coded and segregated*).
   * **Yes / No Items**: Confirm conditions (e.g., *Hand sanitizer dispensers full and operational*).
   * **Numeric Readings**: Enter measurements where prompted (e.g., *Cold storage vaccine refrigerator temperature: 4.2°C*).
   * **Remarks**: If an item fails, type a clear, brief explanation of the problem.
   * **Photo Evidence**: For items marked as **Evidence Required** or if you flag a failure, tap **Take Photo** to snap a photo directly using your phone's camera.
4. **Review & Sign Off**:
   * Verify all required questions have answers.
   * Review the real-time compliance score calculated at the bottom.
   * Tap **Submit Inspection**.
5. Once submitted, the inspection is permanently locked and recorded in the hospital's central dashboard.

---

## 6. Raising Facility Maintenance Complaints

If you encounter physical defects during your inspection (e.g., leaking tap, broken electrical switchboard, damaged patient bed, oxygen line valve alarm):

1. Tap **Complaints** > **New Complaint** (or tap **Raise Complaint** directly from a failed checklist item).
2. Fill in the complaint details:
   * **Building, Floor, and Ward**: Pre-filled if raised from an inspection.
   * **Category**: Choose from `Plumbing`, `Electrical`, `Bio-cleaning`, `Civil`, `Medical Gas`, or `HVAC`.
   * **Priority**:
     * `Low`: Minor cosmetic issue (e.g., scuffed wall paint).
     * `Medium`: Normal maintenance (e.g., flickering tube light in hallway).
     * `High`: Affects patient or staff comfort (e.g., AC malfunction in doctor duty room).
     * `Emergency`: Immediate hazard to patient safety (e.g., water pipe burst near ICU, medical gas pressure drop).
   * **Description**: Explain the exact issue and location in the ward.
   * **Photo**: Attach a clear photo showing the defect.
3. Tap **Submit Complaint**.
4. The system automatically creates a unique reference number (e.g., `CMP-20261014-0012`) and starts the SLA countdown timer for facility engineers.

---

## 7. Updating & Resolving Assigned Complaints

If a complaint is assigned to you to verify or repair:
1. Tap **Complaints** > **My Assigned Complaints**.
2. Tap on the complaint card to open the ticket.
3. Update the ticket as work progresses:
   * Tap **Mark In Progress** when technician arrives at the ward.
   * Tap **Put On Hold** if waiting for spare parts (enter the supplier reason).
   * Tap **Resolve Complaint** once the repair is finished.
     * Enter the **Resolution Notes** (what was fixed and parts replaced).
     * Attach an **After-Repair Photo** as proof.
4. The Hospital Admin will review the resolution and close the ticket.

---

## 8. Shift Check-Out

At the end of your shift:
1. Ensure all scheduled inspections for your shift are submitted.
2. Return to the nursing station or facility office.
3. Tap **Attendance** > **Check Out**.
4. Scan the QR code to confirm departure.
5. Your shift hours and inspection completion metrics will be recorded in the hospital's daily attendance ledger.
